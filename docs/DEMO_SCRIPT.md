# 4–5 minute judge demo

1. **Problem (20 seconds):** Multiple people and AI tools create content, while approved facts and brand rules are scattered. Generic proofreading does not know the organisation's evidence.
2. **Creator workflow (60 seconds):** Sign in as `creator@freshsip.com` / `creator123`. Create a short FreshSip post with a banned term, a wrong product name and a claim such as “cures every cold”. Run the check.
3. **Explainability (45 seconds):** Open findings and show severity, exact source code, supporting guideline text and why an unsupported claim is marked for verification rather than called false.
4. **Human control (40 seconds):** Apply one safe suggested term correction. Explain that the exact span is replaced and other findings are marked stale until the content is checked again.
5. **Review workflow (50 seconds):** Re-check and submit. Sign in as reviewer (`reviewer@freshsip.com` / `reviewer123`), open the queue, add feedback and request changes or approve.
6. **Governance (45 seconds):** Sign in as admin (`admin@freshsip.com` / `admin123`). Show the guideline/fact/term library, source URL/expiry metadata, approved brand palette, visual asset comparison, alt-text check and WCAG AA contrast calculation.
7. **Insights and limits (30 seconds):** Show issue counts and potentially unclear guidelines. State that counts are not accuracy metrics; explain the current limits of rule-based checks, optional external AI and approximate image similarity.
8. **Round 2 validation (20 seconds):** Present actual survey/interview counts and labelled test outcomes from the research log. Do not invent numbers or claim time saved without measurement.
